Support for life's most difficult moments.

ZedCare is designed to help families find and request funeral services, transport, coffins, catering and other practical support from one place.

How ZedCare works

1. Choose a service and submit a request.

2. ZedCare or a service provider confirms availability and price.

3. Agree on details before any payment.

Requests are sent securely to the ZedCare team. You can also reach us on WhatsApp.

Emergency notice: This prototype is not an emergency dispatch service. It does not contact an ambulance or hospital. For an urgent medical emergency, call your local emergency service or a verified ambulance provider directly. Do not wait for this app.

About ZedCare

ZedCare is a proposed Zambia-based coordination platform intended to help people find funeral support and transport services with fewer separate calls.

Planned services

Emergency ambulance coordination, funeral and deceased-body transport, coffins and caskets, funeral catering, tents and chairs, flowers, funeral attendants, announcements and other family support services.

What this version can do

It can display services, send requests online to the ZedCare team, offer WhatsApp contact, show a sample partner directory and export local requests. It is a front-end prototype, not a fully deployed commercial app.

What must be built before public launch

Secure user accounts, provider verification, live request routing, payment gateway integration, privacy notices, backup and security controls, customer support and testing. Emergency dispatch must only be offered with properly licensed and operational partners.
